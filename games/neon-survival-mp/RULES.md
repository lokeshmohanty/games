# Neon Survival

Dodge the red swarm and catch the green gems. Everyone shares one arena, and each gem you catch scores for you.

## How to play
- Every player flies a glowing square ship in the same arena. Play alone with bots filling the other ships, or with friends.
- Red dots fly in from the edges. Each one is aimed at a ship still flying and keeps going in a straight line.
- Touch a red dot and your ship is down. You then watch the rest of the run.
- Green gems appear around the arena, up to three at a time.
- Every gem anyone catches makes the swarm tougher. Red dots arrive more often and fly faster.
- Bots move at a fixed top speed, so a fast swarm can catch them.

## Scoring
- Each green gem you catch scores 1 point for you.
- Your score stays when you go down.

## Winning
- The run lasts as long as any ship, a person's or a bot's, is still flying.
- When the last ship goes down, everyone is ranked by their own score.
- Equal scores share a place.
- Played alone with no other ships, it is simply your best score.

## Example
You, Sam and one bot start a run. A new red dot arrives every second at first. You catch 4 gems, Sam catches 3, and the bot catches 3. That is 10 gems in the run, so red dots now arrive every 0.9 seconds and fly faster than before.

Sam is hit first, then you. The bot keeps flying and catches 2 more gems before it goes down. Final scores: bot 5, you 4, Sam 3. The bot wins. Had the bot stopped at 4, you would have shared first place.

## Controls
- On a phone, put a finger down anywhere and drag. Your ship moves as far as your finger does, in the same direction, so it stays in sight instead of under your thumb.
- Only one finger steers. A second finger does nothing, and your ship stops at the edges of the arena.
- With a mouse, just move it. Your ship follows the pointer; no click is needed.
- There are no keyboard controls.
