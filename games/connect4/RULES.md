# Connect 4

Drop your discs into the frame and be the first to line up four of your colour in a row.

## How to play
- Two players: **red** (Team A) and **yellow** (Team B). Playing alone, a bot takes the other colour.
- The board has 7 columns and 6 rows. Red moves first, then you take turns.
- On your turn, pick a column. Your disc falls to the lowest empty space in it.
- A full column cannot be played; choose another.

## Scoring
- There are no points. A line of four of your discs ends the game at once.
- Four in a row counts across, up and down, or on either diagonal.

## Winning
- The first player to make four in a row wins.
- If all 42 spaces fill up with no line of four, the game is a draw.
- If your opponent leaves the party during the game, you win.
- The end card offers **PLAY AGAIN** for a rematch.

## Example
Columns are numbered 1 to 7 from the left.

1. Red drops in column 4. The disc lands on the bottom row.
2. Yellow drops in column 4 too. Their disc sits on top of red's.
3. Red plays 3, yellow plays 3 (on top of red), red plays 5. Red now has three in a row along the bottom: columns 3, 4 and 5.
4. Yellow must block one end, say column 2.
5. Red drops in column 6. The bottom row now reads red in 3, 4, 5 and 6: four in a row, and red wins.

## Controls
- Tap or click anywhere in a column to drop your disc there.
- On a computer, the column under the mouse is highlighted before you click.
