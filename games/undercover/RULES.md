# Undercover

Everyone gets a secret word, but the undercovers get one that is nearly the same. Describe yours, then vote out whoever seems different.

## How to play
- Play on one phone, passed round the table, with 3 to 12 players. There are no bots.
- Type in the names, choose how many undercovers, and pick topics and difficulty.
- With six or more players you can turn on **BLANK**: one player gets no word and must bluff.
- Pass the phone round. Each player taps to see their word, then hides it. Everyone else looks away.
- Everyone sees the topic. Nobody is told if they are a civilian or an undercover; only the blank knows.
- Each round, in a random order, every player still in says one word out loud about theirs. Never the word itself.
- Argue, agree on one player, and vote them out. The phone shows who they were.
- A blank who is voted out gets one guess at the civilians' word.

## Scoring
- Everyone on the winning side scores, even if voted out: civilians 1 each, undercovers 3 each, the blank 4.
- Totals carry from hand to hand on this phone until **RESET SCORES**.

## Winning
- Civilians win once every undercover and the blank are out.
- Undercovers win as soon as the civilians still in are no more than the undercovers and blank still in.
- The blank wins outright by naming the civilians' word. Spelling must match; capitals do not matter.
- Otherwise, play another round.

## Example
Five players, one undercover, topic Animals. Ana, Cy, Di and Ed get *Lion*; Bo gets *Tiger*. Ana says "mane", Bo says "stripes". The table votes out Bo, the undercover, so the civilians win: Ana, Cy, Di and Ed score 1 each.

Had they voted out civilians instead, two wrong votes leave two against Bo. A third leaves one against one, and Bo wins 3 points.

## Controls
- Everything is a tap; type names in setup, and the blank types a guess.
- **TAP TO SEE YOUR WORD**, then **HIDE & PASS ON**.
- **NEXT** for the next speaker, then **GO TO VOTE**.
- Tap a name, then confirm with **VOTE OUT**.
- After a hand: **PLAY AGAIN**, **CHANGE PLAYERS** or **RESET SCORES**.
