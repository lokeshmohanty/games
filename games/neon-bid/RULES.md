# Neon Bid

Twelve gems go up for auction one at a time. Bid in secret with 100 credits and collect the gems that score you the most points.

## How to play
- There are twelve gems: four colours, three of each. All twelve are shown in order from the start.
- You get 100 credits for the whole game. There is no income.
- For each gem, everyone bids at once, in secret: a whole number from 0 up to your credits. 0 is a pass.
- You have 60 seconds. Bidding closes early once everyone has sealed a bid. No bid counts as a pass.
- The bids are then shown together. The highest bid wins the gem but pays only the **second-highest** bid. A lone bidder pays 1.
- A tie for the highest bid means nobody pays and the gem is lost. So is a gem everybody passes on.
- Bots fill any empty seats.

## Scoring
- One gem of a colour scores 1 point, two score 3, three score 6.
- At least one gem of every colour adds a 4-point rainbow bonus.
- Leftover credits score nothing, but they break ties.

## Winning
- After the twelfth gem, the most points wins.
- A tie on points goes to whoever has more credits left. Still tied, the win is shared.

## Example
You hold one Lime gem and 70 credits. A Lime comes up: a pair would add 2 points. You bid 12, Ana 8, the bot 5. You win and pay 8, leaving 62 credits.

You finish with three Lime (6), one each of the other colours (3) and the rainbow (4): 13 points. If Ana also has 13, whoever has more credits left wins.

## Controls
- Tap **−** or **+** to change your bid by 1.
- Tap a chip (0, 5, 10 or 25) to set your bid to it.
- Tap **BID** (or **PASS** at 0) to seal it. A sealed bid cannot be changed.
- At the end, the host can tap **NEW GAME**.
